Architecture. Diagnosis rules pick a hypothesis from verified facts → the plan is a list of steps, each declaring the facts it depends on → every event updates facts → SignalMind re-checks the remaining plan and replans only when an assumption breaks. Completed steps are preserved. Unverified or destructive suggestions are held for a human.

Two-year thesis (draft). Agents will be judged on how well they re-plan: noticing broken assumptions, separating verified evidence from rumour, and handing control to a human instead of guessing.
